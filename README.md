## 🚀 Playbook 1: Your first automation in 10 minutes

1. **[Create your free Make account](https://www.make.com/en/register?pc=viralwave)** — free tier covers most starter scenarios.

2. Pick one recipe from the catalog below — start with something you do manually every day (e.g. saving email attachments to Drive).

3. Click the recipe link, hit **Use template**, and follow the click-by-click wizard to connect your apps.

4. Run it once manually to verify, then set the schedule and let it run on autopilot.

5. Come back and clone 5 more. That is the whole game.

## 🎯 Playbook 2: The lead-gen machine

- **Capture** — a Lead Generation recipe pushes every new lead into Google Sheets or your CRM instantly.

- **Enrich** — a CRM recipe scores and routes the lead to the right pipeline stage.

- **Follow up** — an Email Marketing recipe fires a personalized sequence within minutes.

- **Alert** — a Team Chat recipe pings you on Slack the moment a hot lead lands.

- **Report** — a Google Workspace recipe rolls every week's numbers into a dashboard.
